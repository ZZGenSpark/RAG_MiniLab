# Time & Usage Policy — Version 1.0

## 1. Purpose

Doofenshmirtz Evil Incorporated recruits a specific kind of employee: brilliant, restless, allergic to sitting still, and structurally incapable of leaving a "quick five-minute break" at five minutes. This policy exists not to suppress that energy but to channel it — into scheduled recreation and a metered token economy — before it channels itself into the server room, the chemical stores, or another employee's personal space. It governs recreational video game time, foosball time, and the allocation, escalation, and depletion of Focus Tokens.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to all full-time employees, contractors, interns, and any reanimated former staff currently carried on active headcount. It does not apply to the Chief Token Officer (CTO), who is, by charter, beyond policy.

## 3. Video Game Time

### 3.1 Daily Allowance

Employees may play video games for up to 45 minutes per workday, taken in increments of no fewer than 15 minutes at a time. This minimum-increment rule exists because of the well-documented "quick five minutes" spiral logged repeatedly in prior incident reports, in which a five-minute break reliably metastasized into ninety.

### 3.2 Suspension Conditions

Video game time is a privilege, not an entitlement, and is automatically suspended for any employee who has set something on fire that day, literally or figuratively.

## 4. Foosball Time

### 4.1 Daily Allowance

Foosball is capped at 20 minutes per employee per day, split across no more than two sessions. The foosball table is company property, a shared resource, and a documented productivity hazard.

### 4.2 Dispute Resolution

Disputes over table access are resolved in favor of whoever is currently winning the game in progress — unless that person is also the one who instigated the dispute, in which case they forfeit the argument automatically, regardless of the score.

## 5. Token Allocation

### 5.1 Allocation Amount

Every employee is issued 1,000,000 (one million) tokens at the start of each six-hour cycle, replenished automatically. Tokens are the operating currency of this company: they are spent on tasks, deliverables, deep thoughts, and, in at least one logged case, a ninety-minute internal debate about whether a hot dog qualifies as a sandwich.

### 5.2 No Rollover

Unused tokens do not roll over between cycles.

## 6. Requesting Additional Tokens

### 6.1 Request Process

An employee who exhausts their allocation before the six-hour cycle resets may submit a request for supplemental tokens to the Chief Token Officer.

### 6.2 CTO Discretion

The CTO evaluates each request on the basis of how important he judges the underlying work to be — a standard that is entirely, and irrevocably, at his sole discretion. There is no appeals process, no scoring rubric, and no committee review. Employees are strongly discouraged from opening a request with "it's for a good cause," as this has never once worked.

## 7. Token Depletion — Consequences

### 7.1 Mandatory Skincare

An employee who runs out of tokens, and whose supplemental request is denied by the Chief Token Officer, must immediately cease all token-consuming activity for the remainder of the cycle and perform skincare instead. This is the sole sanctioned fallback activity, chosen specifically because it requires no tokens, causes no property damage, and keeps idle hands occupied with something other than the foosball table or the chemical cabinet.

### 7.2 Skincare Stations

Skincare stations, stocked with company-approved products, are available at every desk pod. Employees are encouraged to treat this time as genuine recess rather than punishment; several minor breakthroughs in the company's moisturizer-based research have reportedly originated during mandatory skincare sessions.

## 8. Enforcement

### 8.1 Prohibited Transfers

Token balances are tracked automatically and cannot be self-reported.
Borrowing, pooling, gifting, or otherwise transferring tokens between employees is strictly prohibited under this version of the policy.

### 8.2 Escalation

Any employee found circumventing the token system will have their case escalated directly to the Chief Token Officer, whose discretion, as established above, is final and unreviewable.

## 9. Acknowledgment

Continued employment and continued presence in the building constitute acknowledgment that this policy has been read, understood, and — at minimum — grudgingly accepted.
